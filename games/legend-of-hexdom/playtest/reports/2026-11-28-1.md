## Findings

### 1. "Tables, Bosses, Gifts": "taken" badges become huge red blocks over the Adventure table

- **Kind:** unreadable or overlapping. **Severity:** wrong (the only full view of the sidesheet hides most of the Adventure table once a few loot items are taken).
- **Where:** first seen step 68, round 12, between rounds; again at step 80 after Victory. 1728 x 931.
- **Steps:**
  1. Load the journal above up to step 68 (or any game in which two or more gems/Treasures have been taken from the Adventure table).
  2. Press "Tables, Bosses, Gifts".
  3. Look at the Adventure half of the sidesheet.
- **Expected:** each taken item shows the same small red "taken" tag the per-hex panel shows (e.g. step 16, Giant Bat cell: a small tag on the Book). Rulebook p19: "cross them off of the Adventure table".
- **Actual:** six red rectangles, each starting at its loot icon and stretching to the right and bottom edges of the table, covering the Adventure rows from the 3rd column rightwards (screenshot). Measured in the DOM (read only): `span.taken` children of `div.full-table`, sizes 696x175, 372x331, 262x331, 262x18, 153x175 and 46x18 px. The Explore half, the Boss table and the Gift row below are unaffected.
- **Evidence:** `shots/shot-068-sidesheet-taken-red-blocks.jpg`. Log at the time: "69. Explored the grove with a 1: +3 food."
- **Repeats:** yes (reopened at step 80 after Victory: the same red blocks; I did not recount them).
- **Confidence:** certain.

### 2. Boss buttons say "needs the <Treasure>" when you already hold it

- **Kind:** untrue message. **Severity:** confusing.
- **Where:** step 31 (round 7) and step 78 (round 14), main phase in a Castle. 1728 x 931.
- **Steps:**
  1. Replay the journal to step 31 (Castle at row 7 col 3, dice 4, 6, 6; the Book of Shadows was taken at step 16 and shows as a filled dot in the Treasure box).
  2. Select the die showing 4.
- **Expected:** the button tells me whether I meet the Treasure requisite (rulebook p17-18: "if you don't have the specific Treasure required by a Boss when you fight him/her, you lose 3 HP"). With the Book in hand, something like "has the Book of Shadows", or no Treasure note.
- **Actual:** "Boss Brok · needs the Book of Shadows · you would die". At step 78, holding the Crow: "Boss King · needs the Crow · a Gift to choose, Boss defeated". The outcome line is right in both cases (the -3 is not applied), so the wording is the only problem - but "needs" reads as "you are missing it", and at step 31 I had to open the Treasure box to be sure.
- **Evidence:** play log steps 31 and 78; log line 80 "Boss fight with a 6: King. King is defeated, choose a Gift." (no Treasure damage).
- **Repeats:** yes (every Boss button I saw, before and after taking the Treasure).
- **Confidence:** certain.

## Friction (opinions, not bugs)

- The Boss table (requisites and Treasures) is only visible inside "Tables, Bosses, Gifts". While planning a route to a Castle I opened it repeatedly; a small always-visible Boss strip (six requisite pairs + Treasure, ticked when held) would save that.
- In "Tables, Bosses, Gifts" the columns are labelled only by the terrain pictures, as on the paper sheet (`shots/shot-001-tables-dialog-no-headers.jpg`); the per-hex panel names them ("EXPLORE · GROVE"). In the full view I had to match pictures to tell Grove from Forest.
- Map hexes have no row/column labels, but the log and the panel speak in "row 9, col 5". Matching the two needs counting hexes.
- The Enchant switch can be ticked for fights against enemies with no Strength requisite (Ooze, Rat, Evil Champion): the preview honestly shows "-1 heart" or "no change", but it is a trap. The preview is enough; maybe grey those out when Enchant only costs.
- The dice row moves (y 109 before travelling, y 90 after) and the action buttons reflow when a switch is ticked, so the same click position does different things from one step to the next.
- The gem keep/trade prompt and the "Place an attribute point" prompt replace the dice; a stray second click on a placement button places the next point too (I placed several points this way deliberately; an accidental double click would be wrong and needs Undo).
