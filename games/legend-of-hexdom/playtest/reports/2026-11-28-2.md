### 1. A Travel whose hexes all lie in one row leaves no line on the map

- **Kind:** untrue message (the map shows the hexes of that leg as unvisited). **Severity:** wrong.
- **Where:** happened 5 times out of 5 such legs in two games. Viewport 1728 x 931. Game 1 (seed 67739131): step 3 (round 1) and step 79 (round 15). Game 2 (seed 1834211458): step 4 (round 1), step 9 (round 3) and step 25 (round 6).
- **Steps** (game 1, from a fresh Archer on map 1 with this seed; the journal reproduces it):
  1. Roll for the start. The game rolls 3: Village 3 at row 5, col 6.
  2. Roll the dice (2, 1, 5). Select the die showing 5.
  3. Tap Water row 5, col 5, then the Castle at row 5, col 4. Press "Travel to Castle, Plains (row 5, col 4) (-2 food)".
  4. Look at the map.
- **Expected:** a pen line with an arrow from the start X at 5.6 through 5.5 to 5.4, as on every other leg. Rulebook p12: the path is marked "From your starting position". Diagonal legs are drawn, for example step 8's leg 6.4 > 6.3 > 7.2.
- **Actual:** nothing is drawn between 5.6 and 5.4. The log says "Travelled with a 5: 2 hexes to Castle, Plains (row 5, col 4). -2 food." and the leg is in the save (`legs[0].path = ["5.5","5.4"]`), so the rules state is right and only the drawing is missing. The same thing happened on:
  - game 1, step 79: 2.2 > 2.3 > 2.4 > 2.5, row 2;
  - game 2, step 4: 7.5 > 7.4, row 7;
  - game 2, step 9: 5.4 > 5.3 > 5.2, row 5;
  - game 2, step 25: 6.2 > 6.1, row 6.

  Every leg with at least one change of row was drawn, including legs whose first segment is horizontal (game 2, step 60: 9.4 > 9.5 > 8.6 > 7.6). While the path is being planned, the green dashed preview line *is* drawn for these horizontal legs. Only the committed ink line is missing.
- **Why it matters:** on paper the line is the record of which hexes are used. A player reading the map sees 5.5 and 5.4 (or 2.3 and 2.4) as free, plans a path through them, and is refused.
- **Likely cause** (read from the page's DOM, read-only, not from source): the leg is in the DOM as `<polyline class="ink-line" marker-end="url(#arrow)">` with a bounding box of height 0 (e.g. `1034,1005,504,0`). It has `filter: url(#pen)`, and that filter is `<filter id="pen" x="-5%" y="-5%" width="110%" height="110%">`, which is in objectBoundingBox units by default. A zero-height bounding box gives a zero-height filter region, so nothing is painted. Pointy-top hexes never have a vertical segment, so only one-row legs are affected. `filterUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` with absolute margins would fix it, as would padding the region.
- **Evidence:**
  - `shots/g1-step006-map-no-path-line.png`: leg 1 missing.
  - `shots/g1-step008-leg2-drawn-leg1-missing.png`: leg 2 drawn, leg 1 still missing.
  - `shots/g1-step080-row2-leg-not-drawn.png`
  - `shots/g2-step004-one-hex-row-leg-not-drawn.png`
  - `shots/g2-step084-victory.jpg`: the final map of game 2, with 7.5-7.4, 5.4-5.2 and 6.2-6.1 all missing.
- **Repeats:** yes, 5 of 5. **Confidence:** certain.

## Friction (opinions, not bugs)

- **Visited Castles look the same as unvisited ones.** Castles are the scarcest thing on a map: four on maps 1 and 3, and a Travel can't end on a visited one. In both games my second Boss had to be fought in the last unvisited Castle. A visited Castle shows only the thin path line (and not even that, given finding 1), so counting the Castles still usable meant reconstructing my route. Something like a "Castles left: 2" note, or a crossed skull on visited Castles, would have helped me plan. This is a Game Director call, because it adds information the paper sheet does not show.
- **Escape does not close the Menu dialog.** I pressed Escape with the Menu open at the end of game 2, and it stayed open; I had to press Close. I believe the "Tables, Bosses, Gifts" dialog behaved the same way earlier in game 1, but I did not re-check it.
- **Hover names.** The hexes do carry SVG titles (e.g. "Valley (row 5, col 6)"), but they appear only as the browser's native tooltip after a delay, which my screenshots never caught and an iPad never shows. The "If you end at ..." header while a path is drawn is the reliable way to name a hex. I misread one hex's art (5.6 on map 3, which is a Valley, not a Grove) and only found out after travelling.
- **The cell buttons cover the printed icons.** After choosing a die, each lit cell carries a white label ("+1 heart, +1 food, unlocks Overcoming") on top of the printed art. The label is clear, but it hides the printed loot icons, so I could no longer check the label against the print. The list view ("Show as a list") does not have this problem, and I used it when it appeared automatically.
- **Cross Training needs one tap per use.** Six uses in a row in game 1 round 10 meant six taps, each with its own log line. That is fine for honesty, but a "use x times" stepper would be quicker. Low priority.
- **Ruling A (both dice must be used):** I never wanted to leave a die unused. Twice the only safe use of the last die cost a heart (Forest Explore 1-2, -1 heart +3 food, game 2 steps 27 and 37), and both times the food was worth the heart.
