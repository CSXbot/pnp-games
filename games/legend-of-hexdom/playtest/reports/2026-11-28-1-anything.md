# Anything else

Each item is marked **[saw]** (something I observed in play) or **[opinion]**.

## What worked well

- **[saw]** The action buttons are the best part. Every die's options are listed with their outcome
  ("Fight Golden Hare, meet Strength 9, Enchanted · plains - 1 attribute point to place, 1 attribute
  point to place"), and "you would die" stops a fatal click before it happens. I never had to work
  out damage by hand, and every preview I checked matched what the log then reported.
- **[saw]** Unlinked enemies get one button per requisite ("meet Agility 3" / "meet Magic 4"), so
  the choice the rulebook describes on p53 is explicit.
- **[saw]** Path drawing is clear: a green outline for the next step, green dots where the die can
  end, "Keep going" / "Path complete" with the cost, and "Or carry on one more hex (Sail Boat /
  Trusty Donkey)" when the Donkey's extra hex is possible.
- **[saw]** The Travel box in the turn panel highlights which dice you hold. With it I never misread a
  die's cost.
- **[saw]** "Taken" tags in the per-hex cells (not the full sidesheet, see finding 1) and the Treasure
  box with filled dots made it easy to remember what was gone.
- **[saw]** Undo (U) within a round worked, and the journal records only what stands.
- **[saw]** Playtest isolation worked: playtest keys only; the real save untouched.
- **[saw]** The log is complete and in plain words; I could rebuild every number from it.

## Balance thoughts

- **[opinion]** The Mage felt strong on map 2. Enchant + Mana Shield turns every Strength enemy into
  a Magic enemy for 1 food, and the Magic-4/5 food enemies (Rat, Ooze) appear in several columns of
  sidesheet B. By round 4 I had food 20 and Mana Shield. Worth measuring against the Warrior and the
  Archer on the same seeds.
- **[opinion]** Agility was the bottleneck for the Mage's second Boss. The Relic box on sidesheet B
  (Agility +2, Magic +2) and the Crazy Barbarian (2 attribute points) were what made it reachable.
  On sidesheet A or C the Mage may struggle more for the second requisite.
- **[saw]** The Treasure Crate as a Boss reward can finish the game in the same round (I took it
  after Glubo to get King's Crow). That made the Boss round feel very swingy. It is a legal and
  strong play the bot should know.
- **[opinion]** Map 3 has a lot of Mountain in rows 2-6; the Trusty Donkey felt close to required.
  The pocket at row 7 col 6 (only exit a Mountain) is an easy trap.

## Ideas (for the Game Director to weigh; none of these is a rule change)

- **[opinion]** A small always-visible strip with the six Bosses' requisites and a tick when the
  Treasure is held. I opened the full sidesheet several times only to look at the Boss row.
- **[opinion]** Row/column labels (or a hover tooltip) on the map, since the log speaks in "row 9,
  col 5".
- **[opinion]** On a Boss button, say "has the Crow" rather than "needs the Crow" when the Treasure
  is held (finding 2), or show a green tick.

## Tedious

- **[saw]** Placing several attribute points means pressing "Magic 10 -> 11" then "Magic 11 -> 12";
  with 3 Vital Eggs points that is three presses in the same spot. Fine, but an accidental double
  click places two (Undo saves it).
- **[saw]** Explore-only rounds (rounds 11-13 of game 1) are three clicks a die. That is the paper
  game's pace, so no change is needed.

## Questions I could not settle

- **[saw]** Is a fight won with Enchant's lost heart a "perfect combat"? The log says "won without a
  scratch" (finding 4). Nothing in scope depends on it, but the Druid's Wolf Pack would.
- **[saw]** Ruling A (both main dice must be used): in about 20 main phases I never wanted to leave a
  die unused; an Explore with no cost was always there. It never came up as a problem.
