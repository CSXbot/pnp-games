## Rules I checked and found right

I compared each with the rulebook, the sheets and RULES-NOTES.md.

- **[seen] Every preview I checked matched the result and the log.** That is roughly 60 Explore cells, fights, Travels and Boss buttons across the two games. The "you would die" warnings were right each time I worked the numbers myself. Two examples:
  - Spear Bandit by Strength 0 against 4 with 4 hearts.
  - King without the Crow at 4 + 3 = 7 hearts lost out of 7.
- **[seen] Partial payment (p24).** Explore -2 food with 1 food gave "-1 food, short of 1 food, -1 heart, +1 Strength". A 5-Travel at food 0 gave "short of 2 food, -1 heart, -1 heart".
- **[seen] Attribute caps.** The game ignores points above 12 and says so:
  - "Focus: Agility is at 12, 1 ignored."
  - Relic C's completion: "+2 Agility, Strength is at 12, 2 ignored."
  - Forest Strength +2 at 11: "Strength is at 12, 1 ignored."
  - The point buttons grey out the attribute at 12.
- **[seen] Focus (Archer)** fires on the second Adventure of a round only, including when the second fight is the same enemy whose gem was already taken (ruling Q5).
- **[seen] Cross Training.**
  - It shows as a "SKILL: Cross Training" button only before travelling.
  - It can be used repeatedly (ruling C): 6 in a row and later 3 in a row, one log line each.
  - The button disappears when food is below 2, so the cost must be paid in food (consultant's ruling).
- **[seen] Food milestones.**
  - Food 10 gave an attribute point.
  - Food 20 unlocked Cross Training (Archer) and Overcoming (Warrior). Previews said "unlocks Cross Training" and "unlocks Overcoming" before the action.
  - Food 30 gave a point.
- **[seen] Overcoming** (once a round, the first heart lost) showed as "+1 food" in the Striped Bear's preview after it unlocked.
- **[seen] Gifts.**
  - The Balloon let 2s and 6s cross Mountains and Water.
  - The Trusty Donkey let a 3 make a 3-hex path over a Mountain (ruling Q3). After 3 hexes the game said "Path complete" and ignored a 4th tap.
  - The Campfire paid a point after every 3/4/5 Travel ("1 attribute point to place (Campfire)").
  - The Vital Eggs gave 3 points.
  - A taken Gift is never offered again on a later double. With a triple, the third die travels.
- **[seen] Relic boxes.** A (4 gems) gave "+2 Agility, +2 Magic, +2 Strength". C (3 gems) gave Agility +2 and Strength +2. Trade on C gave +3 food.
- **[seen] Bosses.**
  - Previews say "you have the Book of Shadows" or "without the Silver Hammer: +3 hearts".
  - Two Bosses fell in one Castle in one round (game 2).
  - The reward list holds only the untaken Gifts. The Treasure Crate asks for 2 Treasures and names each one's Boss ("for Stuker").
  - The win message after the second Boss is "Second Boss defeated: you win! Use any die still in hand, or finish."
- **[seen] Score.** Both final scores (130 and 130) match my own count from the score line. The hex count was 32 in both games and matches my leg-by-leg sum.

## Balance and the bot

- **[opinion] Castles, not hexes, are the clock that matters.** Maps 1 and 3 have four Castles each. A Castle's Adventure cells are tempting: the Evil Castle Guards' "+1 heart, +1 point" and the Bandit Crew's Crow on C. But every visit removes a possible Boss venue. In both of my games the winning Boss round used the last or second-to-last free Castle. I suspect a good share of the bot's "stuck" losses are Castle exhaustion. Worth logging "free Castles left" at the end of each bot game.
- **[opinion] Count live faces.** The bot plans two fixed target Bosses. A player does better counting how many die faces are live Bosses and adding a third when cheap. The Boss reward is the cheapest way to add one, because it arrives the moment it's needed (rule 14 in the notebook).
- **[opinion] Sidesheet A favours the Archer.** Four Agility-2/3 enemies give free gems early, and the 4-gem Relic's +2 to all three is huge. Map 1's Castle 5.4 is 2 hexes from Village 3 across Water, and its Bandit (Agility 2) is free on round 1.
- **[opinion] Sidesheet C starves the Warrior of food.** Its best Strength cell (Forest 3-4) costs 2 food, and the cheap food cells cost hearts (Forest 1-2). Food sat at 0-3 for the whole of game 2. The Campfire made up for it.
- **[opinion] Archer vs. Warrior.** Both won here. The Archer felt easier: Focus is free Agility, and Cross Training turns surplus food into Strength. The Warrior's Strength comes fast, but his second attribute (Agility 8) costs food he does not have.

## Ideas for the Game Director

These are product choices, not bugs.

- **[opinion] Show which Bosses are live now** (requisites met, Treasure held) on the Boss strip, for example a tick. I recomputed this by hand every round from three attribute tracks and six Treasure slots.
- **[opinion] Show which Castles are still free** (or cross out visited ones). On paper the player's own path marks do this. On screen the line is thin, and missing entirely for one-row legs (finding 1).

## What I enjoyed and what was tedious

- **[opinion] Enjoyed:**
  - The green dots for "this die can end here".
  - The "If you end at ..." preview of the destination's cells while a path is drawn: this is the single most useful control.
  - Honest previews: "you would die", "short of 1 food".
  - A log that says exactly what changed.
  - Tapping the printed cells works well once you know a die must be picked first.
- **[opinion] Tedious:**
  - Six separate taps for six Cross Trainings.
  - Reconstructing my own route to count free Castles.
  - Native tooltips as the only hex names on the map.
