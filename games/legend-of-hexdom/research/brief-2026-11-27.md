# THE GAME

"Legend of Hexdom - a hex and slash adventure", by Papermage Games. A solo-capable print-and-play roll-and-write roguelike. Campaigns and add-ons: Hex Forest (the base, 100 maps), Lost Sands, Winter Tales, Epic-Sized Maps, Bonus Characters. We work from the English rulebook marked version 1.2 (112 A5 pages, file dated May 2024).

Each round the player rolls three six-sided dice. One die is used to Travel across a hex map (paying food). The other two are used for actions: Explore, Adventure (fight an enemy from a table), or fight a Boss in a Castle. A double may instead be spent on a Gift. Beating two Bosses wins. Each character (Warrior, Archer, Mage and others) has three skills. Hearts are hit points; food is both a resource and experience.

# THE QUESTIONS

Page numbers refer to the English rulebook 1.2. Square brackets stand for an icon printed on the card.

**1. May an action die be left unused?** Rulebook: "Here we use the remaining two dice to take actions." (p13). "Travel is mandatory, as heroes don't sit around!" (p34). The Brawler's skill says "this Explore action is mandatory, you cannot avoid it!" (p105). May the player decline to use one or both of the two action dice, for example to avoid an Explore result that costs hearts?

**2. The "stuck" crossing.** Rulebook: "If you get to a point where your only movement option is to cross Mountains or Water, but lack the specific die for that, you may cross them with any other die, paying a penalty of 2 Heart Points for each Mountain or Water crossed in this way." (p41). The example on p42 rolls 2, 4 and 6. Does this apply only when none of the three dice has any ordinary legal move, or may the player choose it for one die while another die could still move normally? Does a die that already allows a terrain (a 4 for Water) avoid the penalty for that terrain?

**3. Sail Boat and Trusty Donkey.** Sail Boat: "You may now use any die to cross Water. When crossing Water, you may also move one additional hex." (p71). Trusty Donkey says the same for Mountains (p72). Is that one extra hex per Travel, or one per Water or Mountain hex crossed? Is the extra hex optional?

**4. A full Food or HP box.** The character card has exactly 50 food diamonds and 32 hearts. Rulebook: "Food points must be painted from left to right, and from top lines to bottom lines." (p23). For attributes only: "After level 12, any attribute point gained is ignored." (p22). The Legacy mode unlocks mention "your Food box filled to the maximum and totally used" and "fill your 40th heart" (p29), although the card has 32 hearts. Once all 50 food diamonds, or all 32 hearts, have ever been painted, are further gains of food or hearts lost? That is: is the box a limit on the total ever gained, not on the amount currently held? Also report any explanation of "40th heart" against a 32-heart card.

**5. An enemy whose gem or Treasure has been taken.** Rulebook: "Treasures can be only taken once. After taking them, cross them off of the Adventure table and mark them in your Treasure box" (p18); "Each Gem can be only taken once. After taking them, cross them off of the Adventure table" (p19). Can that enemy still be fought later (for its remaining loot, or for nothing), or is the whole enemy crossed off?

**6. Scoring "1 point for each hex traveled"** (p25). Does the starting hex count? Do Mountain and Water hexes that were crossed count?

**7. Score after a defeat.** Rulebook: "the game is over, and you win! Time to tally your points!" (p25). In solo play, is a lost game (death, or being stuck) scored at all?

**8. Water drawn two ways.** The Hex Forest maps draw Water either as plain ripples or as a cresting wave (map 1, "The Ranger's Return", has both). The rulebook speaks only of "Mountains and Water". Is there any rules difference between the two drawings, or any published terrain legend?

**9. Warrior, Adrenaline.** Card: "When travelling, if you have 2 [hearts] or less: [Strength] +1". Rulebook: "when traveling, if you have 2 Heart Points or less, gain a Strength point." (p106). Are hearts counted before or after paying the costs of that travel, including hearts lost for food the player could not pay?

**10. Warrior, Magic Blood.** Card: "After travelling, you may [lose a heart] to gain [Magic] +1". Rulebook: "After traveling, you may take one damage to gain one Magic point." (p107). Once per round, or as many times as the player wants to pay?

**11. Archer, Cross Training.** Card: "Before travelling, you may [spend 2 food]: [Strength] +1". Rulebook: "he consumes two food for a Strength point" (p76), and "Active skills can be used at any time, unless otherwise stated." (p22). Only before travelling? How many times per round?

**12. Archer, Magic Berry.** Card: "Every time you improve your [Magic], gain [food]". Rulebook: "earning him one food whenever he improves his Magic." (p77). One food per Magic point gained, or one food per event that raises Magic (a reward of Magic +2 being one event)?

**13. Mage, Enchant.** Card: "When in combat, you can [lose a heart] to use [Magic] instead of [Strength]". The rulebook calls Boss fights "Boss Combats" (p17) but explains Enchant only with Adventure enemies (p94-95). May Enchant be used against a Boss?

**14. Which heart losses count for skills that react to losing a heart?** Warrior, Overcoming: "When you [lose a heart], gain [food]" (once a round). Mage, Mana Shield: "You can [spend a food] to resist [losing a heart] once a round". Do these react only to combat damage, or also to an Explore cost, to hunger (hearts lost for food the player could not pay), to the stuck-crossing penalty, and to self-inflicted costs such as Enchant or Magic Blood?
