# Rulebook 1.3 (Spanish, French) against English 1.2, 2026-11-27

`gemini-4-carbon`, background run, given the three PDFs whole and asked for any change in a rule,
number, timing or limit, any added or removed passage, and a check of every point ruled on in stage
1 (255k input tokens, 5k output). The Game Director's information: no English 1.3 exists; the 1.3
files are the translations (German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese), made 2024-05-30, a day
after English 1.2.

**Conclusion: nothing in scope changed; we implement English 1.2.** The one deliberate rule change
is a clarification to the Survivalist Legacy curse (Villages only, not their underlying terrain).
The rest is removed lore (the Boss pages 109-112), updated sample images, and translation slips.

**Spot-checked by the tech lead** in the PDFs' text: the Survivalist parenthesis on p32 in both
Spanish and French, the English p32 without it, and both 1.3 editions ending at p108. Its other
claims are unverified. Note one slip of its own: it calls the HP box "40-space"; the cards have 32.

---

Based on a page-by-page comparison of the **English v1.2** (pages 1–112), **Spanish v1.3** (pages 1–108), and **French v1.3** (pages 1–108) rulebooks, **version 1.3 made only one deliberate rule clarification** (to the *Survivalist* Legacy curse), **removed the incomplete Boss lore appendix**, and **updated the sample map images on pages 15, 19, and 49**. 

However, the Spanish and French v1.3 editions also introduced **translation errors and mistranscribed symbols** on several character sheets and rule pages that would change gameplay if taken literally, though they are clearly translator/layout mistakes rather than intentional v1.3 design changes.

### 1. Rules, Numbers, Conditions, and Gameplay Differences

#### A. Survivalist Legacy Curse (Deliberate 1.3 Clarification — Both 1.3s Agree)
Version 1.3 clarifies that "cannot interact with Villages" only locks out the Village column itself, not the underlying terrain of that hex.
* **Pages:** EN p. 32 | ES p. 32 | FR p. 32
* **English 1.2:** `"Curse: You cannot interact with Villages."`
* **Spanish 1.3:** `"Maldición: No puedes interactuar con las Aldeas (sólo con su terreno subyacente)."` *(Translation: "Curse: You cannot interact with Villages (only with their underlying terrain).")*
* **French 1.3:** `"Malédiction : vous ne pouvez pas interagir avec les villages (seulement avec les terrains ci-dessous)."` *(Translation: "Curse: you cannot interact with villages (only with the terrains below).")*
* **Agreement:** **Both 1.3 translations agree.**

#### B. The Brainy Traveller & The Brawler Traveller Character Sheet Graphics vs. Text (Translation/Layout Discrepancy — Both 1.3s Agree)
In English 1.2, there is an internal contradiction for *The Brawler Traveller*: the character sheet image says `"travel with the smallest die"`, while the body text below says `"when traveling with a "1" die"` (followed by a note about double low rolls like `2, 2, 4`, proving "smallest die" was intended). In 1.3, the translators left the body text saying `"1"`, and **also** mistakenly changed the *Brainy Traveller*'s double-roll note in French.
* **Pages:** EN p. 102, 104 | ES p. 102, 104 | FR p. 102, 104
* **Brainy Traveller (EN p. 102):** `"In the event of getting double high rolls (e.g.: 3, 5, 5) or triple rolls, this skill is also activated when you travel with one of the highest dice rolled."`
  * **Spanish 1.3 (p. 102):** Matches EN (`"(por ejemplo, 3, 5, 5)"`).
  * **French 1.3 (p. 102):** `"Si vous obtenez un double résultat (par exemple 3, 5) ou un triple résultat..."` *(Drops the second `5`, making the example `(3, 5)` instead of `(3, 5, 5)`).* **1.3s do NOT agree.**
* **Brawler Traveller Character Sheet Box (p. 104):**
  * **English 1.2:** `"GROOVY SPIRIT: travel with the smallest die, [Heart][Crossed-Food]"`
  * **Spanish 1.3:** `"ESPÍRITU GROOVY: viaje con los dados más pequeños, [Heart][Crossed-Food]"` *(plural: "smallest dice")*
  * **French 1.3:** `"L'ESPRIT GROOVY: voyager avec le plus petit dé [Heart][Crossed-Food]"` *(singular: "smallest die")*
  * *(Note: All three versions still say `"1"` in the body text paragraph immediately below the image.)*

#### C. Character Sheet Box vs. Body Text Mistranslations in 1.3
The following differences appear to be translation/typesetting errors in 1.3 rather than rule changes, as the 1.3 body text contradicts the box or vice versa:

1. **The Beast Tamer — *Beast Whisperer* Timing & Duration**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 80–81 | ES p. 80–81 | FR p. 80–81
   * **English 1.2:** Box (p. 80): `"BEAST WHISPERER: [3 crossed food], tame an animal when fighting it"` / Body text (p. 81): `"...as the animal is tamed for the whole match."`
   * **Spanish 1.3:** Box (p. 80): `"DOMADOR DE BESTIAS: [3 crossed food], domestica un animal en la Aventura"` *(tame an animal in Adventure)* / Body text (p. 81): `"...ya que el animal queda domado mientras dure el encuentro."` *(Translation: "...since the animal remains tamed for the duration of the encounter [match mistranslated as encounter].")*
   * **French 1.3:** Box (p. 80): `"DOMPTAGE DES BÊTES: [3 crossed food], apprivoiser un animal lors d'un combat"` *(tame an animal during a combat)* / Body text (p. 81): `"...car l'animal est apprivoisé pour la durée du jeu."` *(Translation: "...because the animal is tamed for the duration of the game.")*
   * **Agreement:** **1.3s do NOT agree** (Spanish mistranslates "match" as *encuentro* [encounter], which would ruin the permanent taming mechanic; French keeps "duration of the game").

2. **The Druid — *Howling Call* Condition**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 86 | ES p. 86 | FR p. 86
   * **English 1.2 (Box):** `"HOWLING CALL: [2 crossed food], gain [Strength +1] in a combat in the wilderness"` *(Note: EN body text just says `"in the Wilderness"` without mentioning combat).*
   * **Spanish 1.3 (Box):** `"LLAMADA AULLANTE: [2 crossed food], gana [Strength +1] en una pelea en la naturaleza"` *(Translation: "...in a fight in nature")*
   * **French 1.3 (Box):** `"APPEL HURLANT: [2 crossed food], [Strength +1] lors d'un combat dans les régions sauvages"` *(Translation: "...during a combat in the wild regions")*
   * **Agreement:** Both 1.3s keep the box's "in combat" requirement and the body text's omission of "in combat", matching EN 1.2's exact ambiguity.

3. **The Mage — *Enchant* & *Mana Shield* Costs**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 94–95 | ES p. 94–95 | FR p. 94–95
   * **English 1.2:**
     * Box (p. 94): `"ENCHANT: When in combat, you can [Crossed Heart] to use [Magic] instead of [Strength]"` / `"MANA SHIELD: You can [Crossed Food] to resist [Crossed Heart] once a round"`
     * Body (p. 95): `"Once per round, she can consume one food to resist one damage..."`
   * **Spanish 1.3:**
     * Box (p. 94): `"ENCANTAR: [Crossed Heart] para usar [Magic] en vez de [Strength] en un combate."` / `"ESCUDO DE MANÁ: puede [Crossed Food] para resistir [Crossed Heart]"` *(**Drops `"once a round"` from the Mana Shield graphic!**)*
     * Body (p. 95): `"Una vez por ronda, puede consumir comida para resistir el daño..."` *(Translation: "Once per round, she can consume food to resist the damage..." — omits the number "one" before food and damage in text, though single icons are in the box).*
   * **French 1.3:**
     * Box (p. 94): `"ENCHANTEMENT: [Crossed Heart] pour utiliser [Magic] au lieu de [Strength] dans un combat"` / `"BOUCLIER DE MANA: vous pouvez [Crossed Food] pour résister à [Crossed Heart]"` *(**Also drops `"once a round"` from the Mana Shield graphic!**)*
     * Body (p. 94–95): On p. 94 example: `"La magicienne utilise l'enchantement et subit des dégâts."` *(omits "one" damage in the example)*; on p. 95 Mana Shield: `"Une fois par round, il peut consommer de la nourriture pour résister aux dégâts..."` *(omits "one" food and "one" damage in text).*
   * **Agreement:** **Both 1.3s agree** in accidentally dropping `"once a round"` from the *Mana Shield* character sheet graphic (while keeping `"Una vez por ronda"` / `"Une fois par round"` in the p. 95 body text).

4. **Boss Combats — Linked Attributes**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 17 | ES p. 17 | FR p. 17
   * **English 1.2:** `"Bosses have two linked attribute requisites and a Treasure requisite each."`
   * **Spanish 1.3:** `"Los jefes tienen dos requisitos de atributo vinculados y un requisito de Tesoro cada uno."` *(Keeps "linked" [vinculados]).*
   * **French 1.3:** `"Les boss ont deux exigences d'attributs et une exigence de trésor."` *(Translation: "Bosses have two attribute requirements and a treasure requirement." — **omits the word "linked"** [liés], though the solid bar is still shown in the Boss table graphic above it).*
   * **Agreement:** **1.3s do NOT agree** (French omission).

5. **Expert Mode Rule 3 — Double Damage**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 27 | ES p. 27 | FR p. 27
   * **English 1.2:** `"3. Everything in the game causes double damage."`
   * **Spanish 1.3:** `"3. Todo en el juego inflige el doble de daño."` *(Translation: "Everything in the game inflicts double damage.")*
   * **French 1.3:** `"3. Tous les objets du jeu infligent des dégâts doublés."` *(Translation: "All objects/items in the game inflict doubled damage." — mistranslation of "Everything" as "Tous les objets").*
   * **Agreement:** **1.3s do NOT agree.**

6. **Gems & Trade — Dice vs. "Data" Typo**
   * **Pages:** EN p. 19 | ES p. 19 | FR p. 19
   * **English 1.2:** `"Entreasuring gems and Trading do not use dice."`
   * **Spanish 1.3:** `"Guardar gemas y hacer cambios no consume datos."` *(Typo: `datos` ["data"] instead of `dados` ["dice"]).*
   * **French 1.3:** `"Sauvegarder des gemmes et faire des échanges ne consomme pas de données !"` *(Translated from the Spanish typo: "does not consume data!").*
   * **Agreement:** Both 1.3s share this translation typo (`datos`/`données` instead of dice).

### 3. Verification of Specific Mechanics Requested

Every single one of the mechanics below was checked across all three files. **None of these rules were changed in 1.3** (save for the Mage graphic omission noted above):

* **Whether the two action dice must be used:** **Unchanged / Still not explicitly stated.** Like EN 1.2 (pp. 12, 13, 34), ES and FR 1.3 explicitly state that **Travel is mandatory** (`"cada uno debe viajar"` / `"chacun doit voyager"`, `"Viajar es imprescindible"` / `"Les déplacements sont indispensables"`), while for the Main Phase (p. 13) they only say `"utilizamos los dos dados restantes para realizar acciones. Estas acciones pueden ser..."` / `"les deux dés restants sont utilisés pour effectuer des actions. Ces actions peuvent être..."` and on p. 25 `"si todavía tienen un dado para usar"` / `"s'il leur reste un dé à utiliser"`. Neither 1.3 adds a rule allowing or forbidding skipping Main Phase dice.
* **The "stuck" crossing over Mountains/Water and its penalty:** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 41–44). Only usable when your **only** movement option is to cross Mountains or Water and you lack the required die; costs **2 HP per Mountain or Water hex crossed** (in addition to normal Travel food cost), and you still cannot stop on Mountains or Water.
* **The Sail Boat and Trusty Donkey:** **Unchanged** (EN pp. 71–72; ES pp. 71–72; FR pp. 72–73). Permanent effect: you may use **any die** to cross Water (Sail Boat) / Mountains (Trusty Donkey), and when crossing them, you **may** also move 1 additional hex.
* **Full Food/HP boxes and the "40th heart":** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 23–24, 29). Neither 1.2 nor 1.3 states a general rule for overflow when the 50-space Food box or 40-space HP box is full, and *Unnatural Health* (p. 29) still says `"fill your 40th heart"` (`"llena tu 40º corazón"` / `"remplir le 40e cœur"`).
* **Enemies whose gem or Treasure was taken:** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 18, 19, 54). Treasures and Gems can only be taken once and are crossed off the Adventure table (`"táchalos de la tabla de la Aventura"` / `"supprimez-les de la table d'aventure"`). No new text was added regarding re-fighting those enemies afterward.
* **Scoring hexes travelled:** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR p. 25). `"1 point for each hex traveled!"` (`"1 punto por cada hexágono recorrido."` / `"1 point pour chaque hexagone parcouru !"`). No change regarding starting hex or Mountain/Water hexes crossed.
* **Scoring a defeat:** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 24, 25, 40). Running out of hearts or getting stuck is still `"game over!"`, and p. 25 still only mentions scoring if all players die in **competitive mode** (`"En el modo competitivo, si todos los jugadores mueren antes de derrotar a dos jefes..."` / `"En mode compétitif, si tous les joueurs meurent avant d'avoir vaincu deux boss..."`).
* **The Warrior (*Adrenaline*, *Overcoming*, *Magic Blood*):** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 106–107).
  * *Adrenaline:* When traveling, if you have 2 HP or less, gain +1 Strength.
  * *Overcoming:* When you take damage, gain 1 food (once a round).
  * *Magic Blood:* After traveling, you may take 1 damage to gain +1 Magic (no "once a round" limit in any version).
* **The Archer (*Focus*, *Cross Training*, *Magic Berry*):** **Unchanged** (EN/ES/FR pp. 21, 76–77). *(Note: On ES p. 21, the English Archer graphic was left untranslated; on ES p. 76 and FR pp. 21, 76 it is translated).*
  * *Focus:* After you Adventure twice in a round, gain +1 Agility.
  * *Cross Training:* Before traveling, you may spend 2 food to gain +1 Strength (no "once a round" limit in any version).
  * *Magic Berry:* Every time you improve your Magic, gain 1 food. *(Minor pluralization quirk in ES p. 77 body text: `"ganar comidas"` ["gain foods"], whereas ES p. 76 box and FR p. 76–77 both show a single food icon / singular).*
* **The Mage (*Enchant*, *Mana Shield*, *Greed*):** **Unchanged in intent** (EN/ES/FR pp. 94–95), except for the **1.3 graphic/translation omissions noted in 1C(3)** above:
  * *Enchant:* In combat, take 1 damage to use Magic instead of Strength (can compare Magic to both requisites if enemy has Strength + Magic).
  * *Mana Shield:* Consume 1 food to resist 1 damage once a round *(as noted above, `"once a round"` was accidentally omitted from the ES/FR p. 94 character box image, though retained in the p. 95 body text)*.
  * *Greed:* Every time you get a gem, gain 1 Attribute point (`+`).
