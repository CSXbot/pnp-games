# Legend of Hexdom: rule readings

How this version reads the rules where the rulebook, the sheets and the cards leave something
open. Each entry says who settled it and when, and what it rests on. The Game Director can overrule
any of them. The evidence is in `research/`: `verification.md` (quotes read on the page, tagged V1-V7
and N1-N9), and the Researcher's blind reading, `2026-11-27-researcher-reading-2.md`. Page numbers
are rulebook 1.2.

## Settled by the rules group, 2026-11-27

The tech lead, the consultant and the Researcher (root `CLAUDE.md`, **Routing**). Unanimous unless
noted. The question numbers are those first put in `SPEC.md` on 2026-11-27.

**Q2. The unstuck crossing.** Offered only when **no die in hand has an ordinary legal path**. Then
any die may be used; each Mountain or Water hex entered without that die's (or a Gift's) permission
costs 2 hearts on top of the die's food, and a hex the die may cross costs nothing extra. The path
still has its exact length, ends on an ordinary hex, and never re-enters a visited hex. *Rests on:*
p41-42; the publisher's comment "This is a rule that should be used ONLY when players are stuck --
not in every move they want." (N1) and "No moves through crossed hexes" (N3). *Sub-case
(consultant's, agreed by the tech lead):* "in hand" means the dice still unspent. If a Gift taken
before travelling leaves one die with no ordinary path, that die may use the unstuck crossing, even
if a die spent on the Gift could have moved normally (p41 read literally).

**Q3. Sail Boat and Trusty Donkey.** When the path crosses at least one Water hex (Sail Boat) or
Mountain hex (Donkey), the player **may** make it exactly one hex longer: one extra hex per Travel,
not per hex crossed, and optional. The two are separate permanent effects, so with both, a path
crossing Water and a Mountain may be up to two hexes longer (live: sidesheets B and C carry both).
*Rests on:* "you may also move one additional hex" (p71-72); the sidesheets' "Travel may: +[boot]";
the Dwarf's card, where Mining triggers "Every time you cross" and Tunneling uses the Gifts'
wording; a player's answer, "you may add one to your movement distance" (V3).

**Q4. The Food and HP boxes are lifetime totals.** A gain paints the next empty diamond or heart;
a spend or a loss slashes or crosses a painted one, which is never freed. **Once the 50th food or the
32nd heart has been painted, further gains of that kind are lost.** In play: a long game can run
out of food for good - every later food cost becomes hearts - and the only relief in the rules is
the Legacy blessing "If your Food box is filled, you no longer spend Food to Travel" (p29). *Rests
on:* p23, p12, p24; the p35 and p42 figures; the publisher's video, "damage isn't erased but you may
gain new Hearts" (V6); Iron Man's "filled to the maximum and totally used" (p29). The "40th heart"
on p29 is a rulebook error against the 32-heart card; it touches only Legacy mode. **Flagged to the
Game Director** as the most consequential of these.

**Q5. An enemy whose gem or Treasure is taken stays.** Only the gem or Treasure is crossed off; the
enemy can be fought again and gives the rest of its loot, or nothing if that was all. It is still a
combat, and an Adventure. *Rests on:* p18-19 ("cross them off", not the enemy, unlike Bosses);
the Beast Tamer, "Your future encounters with that specific animal in that particular position"
(p81); Update #20, "each gem can now be claimed only once per match" (V1), which presupposes meeting
the enemy again; Golden Cradle's curse, "You cannot get Gems from the Adventure table" (p31).

**Q6. "1 point for each hex traveled"** counts every hex the path entered, crossed Mountains and
Water included, and **not** the start hex. *Rests on:* p34, the boots are "the number of hexes you
must travel"; p12, the path is marked "From your starting position".

**Q7. A lost game is not scored.** Death or being stuck is a defeat with no score. A game in which
two Bosses can no longer be beaten goes on until the player dies or is stuck. *Rests on:* p24, p40,
p25 (the tally follows "you win!", and only competitive multiplayer scores players who died); the
publisher, asked whether you keep playing when you can't beat two Bosses: "yes, in this case, you'll
roam the world enjoying the last remaining hextiles before the evil bosses corrupt and destroy all of
Hexia..." (N7); a player's "if you don't beat the bosses you don't win, so you won't get any points"
(V4). *This reverses the tech lead's first proposal* ("shows its score, labelled as a defeat"). What
the defeat screen shows about the run is a product choice for the Game Director, not a rule.

**Q8. Water is Water.** The cresting-wave drawing is decorative. *Rests on:* the rulebook knows only
"Mountains and Water"; one boat icon; the p38 path crosses both drawings alike.

**Q11, timing half. Cross Training only before travelling.** *Rests on:* the card, "Before
travelling, you may", against p22's "Active skills can be used at any time, unless otherwise
stated." How many times is open (below).

**Q13. Enchant works against Bosses.** *Rests on:* the card, "When in combat"; p17 calls them "Boss
Combats"; skills limited to the Adventure table say so (Holy Light, "defeat an enemy in adventure");
the publisher endorsed "maxing out magic/staff first" for the Mage (N6), which only works if
Enchant covers the Bosses that need Strength 12. How Enchant compares is the publisher's own: "treat
the attributes separately, not add them, and treat Swords/Strength as if they were Staves/Magic"
(N5).

**Q14. Every heart lost counts** for Overcoming and Mana Shield: combat, Bosses, an Explore cost,
hunger, the unstuck penalty, and costs the player chooses (Enchant, Magic Blood). Mana Shield
prevents exactly one heart for one food, once a round, and combines with Enchant (N4). *Rests on:*
both cards trigger on the slashed heart, the same mark the Explore table and the skills use; the
publisher's video, "you lose HP in combat exploring or by starving" (V7); p95; p106-107. *The
Researcher was medium, not high, on the unstuck penalty*, because p41 and the Bard (p79) call it a
"penalty" rather than damage.

### Readings taken as settled from the start

Listed in `SPEC.md` on 2026-11-27 and not challenged by the group or the research: starting food 5
(also said in a community playthrough); food milestones count food painted; partial payment; the
path rules; the Village/Castle column chosen per action; linked and unlinked requirements; death
at the last heart; Bosses (a defeated Boss's die can't fight, the Treasure is not spent, two Bosses a
round solo); Gifts (optional, gone once taken, a triple's third die travels); the Treasure Crate;
skills unlocked scoring 0-6; Magic Blood's heart triggering Overcoming; Greed counting every gem.
