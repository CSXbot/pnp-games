## Details

### 1. Board moves when the info panel changes height
**What happens:** The hex board changes position and size between turns. In one game the same building was drawn in different places:
- Food Plant `(0,-2)`: about (287, 290) on day 1, about (278, 300) on day 5.
- New Cryo Tank `(1,0)`: placed by clicking about (407, 412) on day 14. On day 15 it was about (397, 382).

It seems to follow the "Hover a hex / pinned building" panel under the board: when that panel gets taller or shorter (pinned building card vs. placeholder text), the board panel resizes.
**Why it matters:** Click targets move between turns, so it's easy to click the wrong hex when placing a building. Mouse users will notice it too.
**Expected:** The board stays the same size and position. Give the info panel a fixed height, or keep it out of the board's layout.
**To reproduce:** Note where a building is. Hover or pin a hex so the bottom panel shows a building card, then let it go back to the placeholder. Compare the building's position.

### 2. Transceiver distance rule contradicts itself
**Text in the build card:** "Can only be built at least 3 hexes away from other Transceivers (3 hexes in between)."
"3 hexes away" means distance 3. "3 hexes in between" means distance 4. These differ by one.
**Seen:** I placed Transceivers at axial (3,-3), (1,2) and (-3,3), which are 4–6 apart, so I didn't test where the limit actually is.
**Ask:** Check which distance the code enforces and match the text to it, or the code to the rulebook. A test at distance 3 vs. 4 would settle it.

### 3. Game picks which Materials to spend
**Seen:** Building the Laboratory (day 2) took the Materials from building storage first (Separator 3 used, Recycler 2 used) and left the depot's 6 untouched. Later, a Transceiver was paid with an Automatron ("−1 Automatron (as Materials)") with no prompt.
**Why it matters:** Where a resource comes from can matter: storage that is drawn and spent is permanent, depot boxes are limited, and Automatrons have other uses. The player has no say.
**Ask:** If the paper rules let the player choose, add a payment choice or at least a sensible default. If not, write the rule in RULES-NOTES.md.

### 4. A full Food Plant may be highlighted as a Produce target (not confirmed)
**Seen:** Day 10, Produce with a 2 AP die. The Food Plant had drawn 6/6, so no free space, yet it looked highlighted next to its linked Cryo Tank. I clicked the Cryo Tank, so I don't know whether clicking the Food Plant would have been accepted.
**Ask:** Check that only buildings with free space are highlighted. The rule text says "at a single building with free space".

### 5. Double 1s: the merged 2 AP die can't be combined
**Seen:** Day 7, rolled 1, 1, 6. Log: "Double 1s: the two 1s become one 2 AP die, plus your 6; no accident this turn." The 2 AP die was locked, so it couldn't be combined with the 6.
**Ask:** If that's the paper rule, say "can't be combined" in the log or tooltip. If not, it's a bug.

### 6. "Days survived: 16" after a day-17 launch
The win dialog says "You launched the Depot shuttle on day 17!" and then "Days survived: 16". That's probably correct (16 full days), but it reads like an off-by-one. Consider "Launched on day 17" alone, or "Days completed: 16".

### 7. Salvage skips choosing a hex
With one Separator, Salvage applied itself straight away. Produce and Recycle always asked for a hex, even when only one was valid. Either is fine; pick one behaviour.

### 8. Planet 1 shuttle win is too easy (one game, so a weak signal)
Won on **day 17 of 75**, using only 6 of 15 tech/consumption boxes and never close to losing. The route:
1. Build the Laboratory early (day 2). The 3 instant Automatrons cover most Materials costs.
2. Craft 3 more Automatrons. A full Laboratory gives a free tech: **+3 Power**.
3. Study ×3 (plus Research Site full and 1 accident). Depot Sanity reaches box 8: second **+3 Power**.
4. Workshop, then a Battery from any single 6 die (6 AP, no other cost). Transceivers cost 1 AP each.
5. Cryo Tank next to the Oxygen Plant to keep Oxygen at 5 or more.

**Observations:**
- **Power** is the only real constraint, and the Lab's free tech plus a few Studies pay for almost all of it (6 of the 9 spent).
- **Materials** never mattered once Automatrons existed. Separator and Recycler setup actions went unused.
- **Batteries** cost only AP once the Workshop exists, and each adds +1 Radiation protection, so the endgame is a formality.
- **The 75-day survival win** is irrelevant on this map: the shuttle comes much sooner than consumption becomes dangerous.
- **Accident die choice** is the most interesting decision in the game. Keeping a 1 is safe when 2–7 can't hit the resource you're short of.

**Possible changes (to try, not prescriptions):** give Craft Battery a Materials or Power cost; make the Laboratory's free tech something other than Power; or require more Batteries or Transceivers on Planet 1.
